Annadata connects restaurants, hotels, cafes and canteens with verified NGOs and volunteers, so surplus food reaches people who need it.

How it works

1

Post

A restaurant lists surplus food with quantity, servings and a pickup deadline.

2

Claim

Verified NGOs nearby see it, nearest first. First to claim gets it.

3

Collect

The NGO picks up the food. Contact details are shared only after a claim.

4

Distribute

The NGO records how many people were served. Everyone sees the status.
